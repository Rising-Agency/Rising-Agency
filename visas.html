05 · Important

PROFILE READINESS,
NOT LEGAL ADVICE.

Does the evaluation tell me that I legally qualify for a visa?

No. Rising’s evaluation is a professional career, evidence and readiness assessment. It is not a legal determination of eligibility.

What result will I receive?

Your assessment may describe your profile as Strong Profile, Developing Profile, or Profile Requires Further Development, together with the reasons and recommended next steps.

What happens if legal review or filing is needed?

When legal advice, petition preparation by counsel or filing is required, Rising may coordinate with qualified U.S. immigration attorneys.

Is approval guaranteed?

No. No profile evaluation, petition strategy or service can guarantee petition approval or visa issuance.
