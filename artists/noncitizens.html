Biography

Noncitizens bring together afro, indie and melodic influences in a sound shaped for destination events, open-air dancefloors and international rooms. Their identity balances rhythmic warmth with a polished melodic sensibility, creating sets that can move naturally from sunset environments into late-night club energy. With a strong connection to Ibiza and the Rumors orbit, the project continues to expand through international touring and carefully curated appearances.
