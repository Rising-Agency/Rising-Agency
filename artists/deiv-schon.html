Biography

Buenos Aires born and now based in the United States, Deiv Schon approaches the dancefloor through groove, patience and movement. His sets move between house, minimal and indie dance, adapting naturally to the room rather than following a fixed formula. From intimate clubs to open-air gatherings and the Burning Man circuit, his identity is rooted in long-form storytelling, warm low-end and a direct connection with the crowd.
