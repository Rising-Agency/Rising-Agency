Biography

Based in Ibiza and touring worldwide, Igor Marijuan is a DJ, curator and music director whose sets are guided by the crowd, the moment and the environment. His musical language is rooted in house, deep house and indie dance, delivered with the optimistic energy and human connection that define his performances. Beyond the booth, Igor develops projects that bring music, culture and community together, extending his role from selector to curator and creative connector.
