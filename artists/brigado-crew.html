Biography

Formed by Brian Harrison and Martín Kasall, Brigado Crew emerged from Buenos Aires with a sound that connects melodic techno, indie dance and forward-looking electronic music. Their breakthrough “Zulu” reached #1 on Beatport’s Deep House chart, while their catalog has appeared on labels including Diynamic, Stil vor Talent, Tomorrowland Music, Bedrock, Radikon and Toolroom. Their debut album Symbiosis later evolved into their own label and showcase concept, extending the duo’s vision beyond the studio and onto international dancefloors.
