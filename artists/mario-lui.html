Biography

Caracas-born and San Diego based, Mario Lui brings together deep house, Latin grooves, minimal textures and progressive storytelling. As a co-founder of AMA Sounds, his work is closely connected to community and the underground electronic culture of the American West Coast. His sets favor warmth and progression over quick impact, creating a fluid narrative designed for clubs, open-air spaces and long-form dancefloors.
