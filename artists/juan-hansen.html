Biography

Juan Hansen is a composer, producer and live electronic artist from Buenos Aires. Rather than approaching electronic music as a conventional DJ set, Hansen builds performances in real time through vocals, instruments and live composition, moving freely between indie electronic, melodic club music and song-driven moments. His career has taken him through Boiler Room, Watergate, Ultra Buenos Aires and Cosquín Rock, while collaborations and remixes have connected his singular live language with a growing international audience.
