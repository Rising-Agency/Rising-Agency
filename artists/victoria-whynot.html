Biography

Victoria Whynot is an Argentine DJ and producer from Villa Carlos Paz, Córdoba, whose musical identity connects classic house foundations, Detroit influence and indie dance. Beginning her musical journey at a young age, she developed a club language informed by figures such as Frankie Knuckles, Larry Levan and Kevin Saunderson while building a distinctly contemporary voice. Her international path includes appearances around ADE, Pacha Ibiza, Creamfields, Paradise and Lollapalooza, alongside some of electronic music’s most recognized artists.
