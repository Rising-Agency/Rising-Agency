Biography

Argentine-born and Miami-based, Nico Moon is a DJ, producer and event curator whose sound moves through house, deep grooves and indie dance. A resident of RESISTANCE Miami, he has built his profile across clubs and festivals while developing The Hall, his own label and event platform. Recent highlights include consecutive appearances at Lightning in a Bottle, where his sets have helped establish him as an emerging name within the U.S. electronic circuit, alongside performances across Miami, New York, California, Europe and beyond.
